# HAYAT Architectural Decisions & Assumptions Log

This document records all design, privacy, security, and algorithmic decisions made during the autonomous full-stack development and backend integration of HAYAT for the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge (Abu Dhabi).

---

## 1. Core Principles & Privacy Guarantees
- **On-Device Single Source of Truth**: The TypeScript risk engine (`src/engine/riskEngine.ts`) remains the definitive clinical screening engine. The front-end works 100% offline without backend availability.
- **Strict Data Minimisation**: Zero personally identifiable information (no name, national ID, phone, email, free text, or raw answers) ever leaves the device.
- **Backend Role**: The FastAPI backend is strictly an optional sidecar for:
  1. AI plain-language rewrite of screening explanations (via Gemini 2.5 Flash / official `google-genai` SDK) using strictly enum-coded factor tokens.
  2. Synthetic evidence cohort verification and assumptions serving.
  3. Voluntary anonymised research aggregates (strictly bucketed enums with day-level dates).
  4. Health check.
- **Opt-In AI Consent**: A separate, default-OFF consent toggle ("aiExplanation") is required before calling `/api/explain`.
- **Client-Side Document Generation**: The Clinician Summary PDF is rendered locally via print stylesheet and on-device formatting, never sent to or generated by any server.

---

## 2. Risk Engine Parity & Modelling Assumptions
- **Python Risk Engine (`backend/app/engine`)**: Exact mathematical mirror of `src/engine/riskEngine.ts` including score weighting, age factor, conception duration thresholds (active trying only), lifestyle/heat multipliers, BMI bands, and WHO 2021 semen parameter thresholds.
- **Deterministic Red-Flag Safety Override**: Any presence of acute testicular red flags (`severe_pain`, `rapid_swelling`, `fever_pain`, `blood_in_semen`, `hard_painless_lump`) triggers an absolute rule override to `Urgent assessment` regardless of lifestyle or metabolic scores (100% sensitivity by rule design).
- **Parity Verification**: 500 seeded synthetic profiles verified across TypeScript and Python engines asserting 100% identical outputs for `band`, `score`, and `triage`.

---

## 3. Gemini AI Integration & Safety
- **Model Selection**: Uses Google's official `google-genai` Python SDK (`from google import genai`). Default model is `gemini-2.5-flash` (or latest Flash-class discovered dynamically from `client.models.list()`).
- **Safety Post-Filter**: Rejects any AI-generated response that contains diagnostic assertions, alarmist language, stigmatising terms ("infertile", "impotent", "sterile", "diagnos*"), or marital/religious assumptions ("your wife", etc.).
- **Graceful Fallback**: If `GEMINI_API_KEY` is missing, rate-limited, or times out (>10s), the service gracefully returns the deterministic template with `ai_used: false` and HTTP 200 (never crashing or returning 500).

---

## 4. Security & Storage Policies
- **CORS Allowlist**: Restricted to configured origins (`http://localhost:3000`, `http://127.0.0.1:3000`, `http://localhost:5173`, and production URLs).
- **Rate Limiting**: Configured at 20 requests/minute per client using `slowapi`.
- **Anonymised Research Storage**: Stored in Google Cloud Firestore via `firebase-admin` when credentials are provided; falls back gracefully to a sandboxed local SQLite database (`backend/data/research_aggregates.db`) for dev/offline verification without storing any IP or user identifier.
